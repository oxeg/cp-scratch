# Bonus ideas

Finished all three lessons — or finished a lesson early and worked through
the challenges for it? If you've been through `reference/extra-challenges.md`,
maybe more than once, and you're still waiting for the rest of the room to
catch up, this page is for you. It doesn't matter which week you're in.

It isn't a lesson, and it isn't the challenges sheet either. No numbered
steps, no milestones, no "Check it works", no "Stuck?" — none of the shape
you'd expect from either of those. That's on purpose. This page isn't
unfinished; it's a different kind of page.

It has no difficulty stars, on purpose too. A star means somebody worked
out roughly how big a job is before you started it. Nobody has done that
for anything below — that's the whole difference from the challenges
sheet. If you want a job with a known size and a hint to go with it, that's
`reference/extra-challenges.md`. If you want something with no size at
all, stay here.

What follows is five directions, not five jobs. Each one is a place to
head towards, not a thing to build. There's no hint attached to any of
them, no block named, no category pointed at — working out what a
direction even means, before you work out how to build it, is the whole
point this time.

> **Give it something else to collect.** A second thing, earned a
> different way from cookies.
>
> **Make the cookie notice something.** Right now it knows *that* you
> clicked. Can it know how fast, or how long since the last one?
>
> **Sell something that isn't a Grandma.** Every shop item so far does the
> same job — makes cookies happen by itself. Add one that does something
> else.
>
> **Make something go wrong.** Every good game has a way to lose, or at
> least a way to be unlucky.
>
> **Make it a different game.** Same machinery, different pictures and
> words. A pizza clicker. A cat clicker. Whatever you'd rather be making.
> Change the costumes and the wording, but if you haven't done week 3 yet,
> keep the names `Cookie`, `cookies` and `Grandma` exactly as they are —
> next week's cards name all three, over and over, and they can't rename
> things for you.

Two things before you start.

The three rules on `reference/extra-challenges.md` still apply here, in
full — nothing on this page is checked or exempted the way an entry there
can be. Read them again if you're doing this before week 3 especially:
rule three is the one that matters most from here, because a direction on
this page can wander straight into shop or golden-cookie territory without
meaning to.

And nobody has built any of these — not the five directions, not a sixth
one you think up yourself, not whoever wrote this page. There's no worked
version sitting somewhere to check your answer against, and no answer to
be wrong about. If you finish all five, that isn't the end of the page —
it means you've got five things to make wider, or a sixth direction of
your own to go chasing. This page doesn't run out.
