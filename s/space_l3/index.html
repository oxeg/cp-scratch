Milestone 3 of 4further targetsprites: Ship, Asteroid

Getting harder

On Ship, add level and spawn gap variables and a script that raises the level and shortens the gap every time score passes level × 10 — then hand Asteroid's spawn timing over to spawn gap.

New blocks

Operators
