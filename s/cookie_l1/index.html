Milestone 4 of 4bonus — if you get theresprite: Special Cookie

Bonus round

Duplicate your cookie, shrink it, park it in a corner, and make it worth ten.

What's new

No new blocks. You duplicate the sprite you already made, then take two things off the copy: its green-flag script, and its sound-and-squash blocks. Remember that dragging a block takes everything below it too.

The copy ends up like this

when this sprite clicked
change cookies by 10

Two blocks. Nothing else — and the same cookies variable as the big one.

✓

The small cookie jumps by 10. The big one still goes up by 1, with its sound and squash. The small one stays small.
