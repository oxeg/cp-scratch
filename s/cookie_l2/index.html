Milestone 1 of 4sprite: Cookie

Open the shop

Open last week's project, add a shopkeeper called Grandma, and make two new numbers for the shop to use.

What's new

No new blocks. You add a sprite and rename it Grandma, make two variables — per second and grandma cost — and set them both at the green flag.

Pick any person sprite you like from the library: a dancer, a wizard, a footballer. Whoever you pick is your shopkeeper, and nobody else's has to match yours.

Every number in this game gets set in this one script, at the moment the game starts.

Cookie — add to the green-flag script you already have

when green flag clicked
set cookies to 0
newset per second to 0
newset grandma cost to 50

Not the click script — the green-flag one. Four blocks when you're done.

✓

Press the green flag. cookies and per second both show 0, grandma cost shows 50, and your Grandma is standing off to one side.

Milestone 2 of 4sprite: Grandma

Buy a Grandma

Click her, and if you can afford her she costs you cookies, earns you one a second, and doubles her own price.

Not less than looks odd on purpose. You want "if I have at least enough" — and Scratch has no greater than or equal to block, so you say it the other way round.

The three blocks go in the mouth of the if, not underneath it.

Grandma — her whole script, and her only one

when this sprite clicked
if not cookies < grandma cost then
set cookies to cookies - grandma cost
change per second by 1
set grandma cost to grandma cost * 2
✓

Green flag, then click her straight away: nothing happens, and that's right. Now click up past 50 and click her again — cookies drops by 50, per second becomes 1, grandma cost becomes 100.

Milestone 3 of 4sprite: Cookie

Cookies bake themselves

A third script for Cookie, standing on its own. Every second it pays you whatever per second says.

New blocks

forever
Control

A sprite is allowed more than one when green flag clicked. Press the flag and every one of them starts, all at the same time.

Look at the bottom edge of a forever: no bump. Nothing can ever be attached under it — it is always the last block in its script.

Cookie — a third script, well clear of the other two

when green flag clicked
forever
wait 1 seconds
change cookies by per second

Both blocks sit in the mouth. The flat foot is the forever telling you nothing goes below it.

✓

Green flag, click up past 50, buy a Grandma, then put your hands in your lap. The counter keeps going up on its own, one every second.

Milestone 4 of 4everyone should reachsprite: Baker

Second shop item

A second helper, ten times as good and ten times the price. You duplicate Grandma rather than building him again.

What's new

No new blocks. One new variable, baker cost, then a duplicate of the Grandma sprite renamed Baker — and four edits to the copy.

The name on a set block is a dropdown, so you can just point it somewhere else. The three orange ovals are not: each one is dragged out onto the palette and a baker cost oval dropped into the hole it leaves.

Clicking 500 times is hard on a finger. Test it with baker cost set to 20, then put it back to 500.

Cookie — one more block on the green-flag script

when green flag clicked
set cookies to 0
set per second to 0
set grandma cost to 50
newset baker cost to 500

Baker — the copy of Grandma, edited

when this sprite clicked
if not cookies < baker cost then
set cookies to cookies - baker cost
change per second by 10
set baker cost to baker cost * 2

No grandma cost left anywhere in it, and a 10 where Grandma has a 1.

✓

Clicking the Baker takes 500 away, adds 10 to per second, and pushes baker cost to 1000. Grandma still costs whatever she was up to.
