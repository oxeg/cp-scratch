Milestone 3 of 4further targetsprite: Cookie

Make the rush obvious

A sugar rush is a number changing in the corner of the stage. That is not much of a party. Give it a sound, and one signal you can see from across the room.

New blocks

start sound the sound you picked
Sound
say Hello! for 2 seconds
Looks
switch backdrop to a backdrop of yours
Looks

Both signals are edits to the when I receive sugar rush script you built in Milestone 2. Pick one — you only need one. Come back for the other if there's time.

Sound and backdrops are your pick from the library. Use whatever name your own Sounds and Backdrops tabs show, and point the dropdowns at that.

The say block holds up the script for its whole two seconds, so the rush runs about twelve seconds instead of ten. Nobody is counting.

Signal one — a speech bubble

when I receive sugar rush
set bonus to 2
newstart sound the sound you picked
newsay SUGAR RUSH! for 2 seconds
wait 10 seconds
set bonus to 1

The say goes under set bonus to 2, so the doubling starts straight away.

Signal two — a second backdrop

when I receive sugar rush
set bonus to 2
newstart sound the sound you picked
newswitch backdrop to your rush backdrop
wait 10 seconds
set bonus to 1
newswitch backdrop to your normal backdrop

Two switch backdrop blocks: one to change it, one at the very bottom to change it back.

✓

Click a golden cookie. The sound plays, and either a bubble appears or the whole stage changes — so you know a rush has started without looking at the numbers. Ten seconds later it all goes back.

Milestone 4 of 4further targetsprite: Cookie

Cookie Master

One more broadcast, to celebrate getting past a thousand cookies. It fires once a game, and once is right.

New blocks

wait until
Control
>
Operators

wait until does what it says: it stops and waits, checking over and over, and the moment the condition comes true it lets the script carry on.

Then the broadcast runs once, the script runs out of blocks, and that is the end of it for the whole game. Nothing has to stop the message coming back.

Waiting for 1000 is slow to test. Put 9 in the > block, click ten times, then put 999 back.

Cookie — the one that watches

when green flag clicked
wait until cookies > 999
broadcast cookie master

cookies on the left, 999 typed on the right. The other way round it is true from the moment the game starts.

Cookie — the one that celebrates

when I receive cookie master
say Cookie Master! for 3 seconds

Check that dropdown says cookie master, not sugar rush — answering the wrong shout is the usual bug here.
